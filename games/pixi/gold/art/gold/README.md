# Vault background

`vault.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. It is the earlier background,
kept as a previous version; the stage now draws the SpriteCook art in `spritecook/`.
The character generation was rejected by the service, which is why the character and the
bag come from SpriteCook instead.

Final background prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Game background asset for a polished portrait mobile game Goat Gold. Square illustration, beautiful high quality stylized 3D animated movie aesthetic, warm golden sunlight. Ancient medieval treasure vault courtyard with weathered sandstone arches, ivy, lanterns and gold coins piled at outer edges. A deep dark bottomless chasm runs vertically down the exact CENTER of the image, opening starting at y=65% and extending to bottom edge. Two stone platforms on left and right at y=72%, their inside edges at x=32% and x=68%, suitable for a character straddling this gap. Center upper area open with atmospheric blue sky through high arch, shafts of sunlight, uncluttered space for falling treasure and character. View frontal slightly looking down on platform tops. No character, no bag, no UI, no lettering. Rich detailed cinematic render, amber and teal, soft depth, platforms clearly separate.
